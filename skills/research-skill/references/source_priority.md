# Source Priority and Conflict Rules

## Priority
Use sources in this order whenever possible:
1. Official company site / location page / legal entity page
2. Official annual report / sustainability report / investor filing
3. Official product brochure / technical document / corporate presentation
4. Official company press release / newsroom
5. Government registry / investment authority / official industrial park source
6. Other reliable third-party sources only when official evidence is insufficient

## Entity and site evidence
For legal entity names, prefer company filings, official legal notices, official site pages, government registries, or annual reports.
For plant function, prefer official location pages, annual reports, official press releases, or site-specific official documents.

## Conflict resolution
1. Prefer newer evidence when status has changed.
2. Prefer site-specific evidence over group-wide generic statements.
3. Prefer legal/registry evidence for registered names over marketing names.
4. Do not combine conflicting facts into one record; preserve the conflict in Remark/Review.
5. If no source can resolve a material conflict, mark REVIEW_REQUIRED.

## URLs
Retain concrete source URLs for traceability.
If third-party information is used, keep the original source URL rather than a vague label such as “media report”.
